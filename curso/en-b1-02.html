Gramática: past simple, past continuous e used to

Uma história no passado mistura fatos, cenário e costumes. Em português, o imperfeito ("tocava") faz dois desses papéis; em inglês, cada um tem a sua forma.

Past simple (-ed ou irregular)Past continuous (was / were + -ing)Used to (+ forma base)
AfirmativaThey played 30 songs.They were touring.He used to play drums.
NegativaThey didn't play.They weren't touring.He didn't use to play.
PerguntaDid they play?Were they touring?Did he use to play?
Para quêfato concluído (tocou)ação em andamento (estava tocando)hábito antigo que acabou (costumava tocar)

O past simple conta os fatos, um depois do outro: The band came on stage, played for three hours and said goodbye. O past continuous (was com I, he, she, it; were com you, we, they) mostra uma ação "no meio do caminho":

Interrompida por uma ação curta (when + past simple): I was listening to Sepultura when the power went out. → Eu estava ouvindo Sepultura quando a luz acabou.

Duas ações longas ao mesmo tempo (while): While the band was playing, the fans were singing. → Enquanto a banda tocava, os fãs cantavam.

Cenário de uma história: It was raining, and people were waiting outside the venue. → Chovia, e as pessoas esperavam do lado de fora.

O used to + forma base fala de costume ou estado do passado que não existe mais:

I used to play guitar every day. / There used to be a record store here. → Eu tocava guitarra todo dia. / Tinha uma loja de discos aqui (hoje não tem mais).

Irregulares desta lição (base – passado): be – was/were · come – came · go – went · have – had · make – made · give – gave · break – broke · fall – fell · ring – rang.

Imperfeito: hábito ou cena? "Eu jogava videogame todo sábado" = I used to play video games every Saturday. "Eu jogava videogame quando minha mãe chegou" = I was playing video games when my mom got home.

When x while: when costuma vir com a ação curta; while, com a longa. E repare: When the teacher came in, we stopped talking (ela entrou e aí paramos) x When the teacher came in, we were talking (já estávamos conversando).

Armadilha 1: verbos de estado (know, like, want, need, believe, understand) não vão para o continuous: ✘ I was knowing the answer. ✔ I knew the answer.

Armadilha 2: com did, used vira use, porque o did já marca o passado: ✔ I didn't use to, Did you use to...? E não existe used to no presente: ✘ I use to study at night. ✔ I usually study at night.

Armadilha 3: be used to + -ing é outra coisa, "estar acostumado a": I'm used to studying at night. ✘ I was used to play in a band. ✔ I used to play in a band.

Leitura: Rock stories (notícias de novembro de 2026)

The end of an era. On November 7, 2026, Sepultura played its farewell show at the Suhai Music Hall, in São Paulo, for about 9,000 people. At first, the concert was going to be at the Pacaembu stadium, with about 26,000 seats, but in September it moved to this smaller venue. The setlist had 30 songs, with some rarities, and the show lasted almost three hours. Several guests joined the band on stage, including João Gordo, with Ratos de Porão, and Yohan, Andreas Kisser's son. Max and Iggor Cavalera used to play in the band's classic lineup, but they turned down the invitation. The encore ended with "Ratamahatta" and "Roots Bloody Roots". Later, while he was taking questions from the audience at a festival in Ravenna, Italy, Iggor talked about the farewell: for him, it isn't really the end, just the end of that version of Sepultura.

Maiden in the Hall. On November 14, one week after the Sepultura farewell, Iron Maiden finally made it into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame, at a ceremony in Los Angeles. But the band members weren't there: they were touring at the time. Jack Black inducted them, and a supergroup with Kirk Hammett and Rob Trujillo (Metallica), John 5, Brad Wilk and St. Vincent played "The Trooper". Phil Collins went up on stage with a cane, but he joked that it wasn't as bad as it looked. Oasis didn't show up either, and Rick Rubin gave a one-sentence speech: "You can't kill rock 'n' roll."

Dica de pronúncia

Used to se fala "IÚS-tâ": s de "sapo" e o d some, colado no t (use to, na negativa e na pergunta, soa igual). Já o verbo to use (usar) tem z: "iúz". No past continuous, was e were ficam rápidos e sem força: I was playing = "ai uâz PLÊI-in(g)". E o -ing termina num n feito no fundo da boca, como o de "banco", sem soltar o g: nunca "PLÊI-in-gui".
