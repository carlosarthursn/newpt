Gramática: presente simples (negativa e perguntas)

Na Lição 7, o presente simples apareceu só na afirmativa (I like rock. She likes rock.). Para negar e perguntar, entra o auxiliar do (com he, she, it, does), que não tem tradução: só carrega o "não" e marca a pergunta. E o does leva embora o -s da 3ª pessoa, então o verbo principal volta à forma básica.

Negativa: sujeito + don't / doesn't + verbo na forma básica

SujeitoAfirmativaNegativaContraída (a mais usada)
I / you / we / theyI like metal.I do not like metal.I don't like metal.
he / she / itShe likes metal.She does not like metal.She doesn't like metal.

Pronúncia: don't (dôunt, sem "i" no fim) e doesn't (DÂ-zânt). A forma longa fica para a escrita formal e para dar ênfase; no dia a dia, use a contraída.

I don't like pop. (Não gosto de pop.) · He doesn't play the guitar. (Ele não toca guitarra.) · The song doesn't have a video. (has volta a ser have)

Perguntas de sim ou não: Do / Does + sujeito + verbo na forma básica? Na resposta curta, repita o do / does, nunca o verbo principal:

PerguntaSimNão
Do you like Ghost?Yes, I do.No, I don't.
Does she play the drums?Yes, she does.No, she doesn't.
Does the station play Sepultura?Yes, it does.No, it doesn't.

Como no to be, a resposta curta afirmativa não se contrai (Yes, I do.). O do soa "dú" e o does, "dâz". E guarde a melodia: na pergunta de sim ou não, a voz sobe no fim; na pergunta com question word, desce, como numa afirmativa.

Perguntas com question words: question word + do / does + sujeito + verbo? É a mesma pergunta de sim ou não, com a palavra interrogativa na frente:

Question wordSignificadoExemplo
what (uát)o que; qual (what kind of = que tipo de)What kind of music do you like? — Rock and metal.
who (hú)quemWho do you listen to? — Ghost and Muse.
where (uér)ondeWhere do the Foo Fighters play in Brazil? — In BH and São Paulo.
when (uén)quandoWhen does the tour start? — In March.
why (uái)por queWhy do you like metal? — Because it's loud!
how (háu)comoHow do you listen to music? — On the radio.
which (uítch)qual (entre opções)Which band do you prefer, Metallica or Megadeth? — Megadeth.
how many (háu MÉ-ni)quantos, quantasHow many dates does the tour have? — 19.

Why x because. "Por que" e "porque" soam iguais em português; em inglês, why pergunta e because responde: Why do you like Ghost? Because the songs are great. E a preposição de verbos como listen to vai para o fim: Who do you listen to? (Quem você ouve?)

Com to be, nada de do: Are you a rock fan? I'm not a pop fan. (= I don't like pop.) Já o do que é verbo principal ("fazer") convive com o auxiliar: What do you do? (O que você faz? Ou seja, qual é a sua profissão?)

Pergunta sobre o sujeito, sem do. Se who é o próprio sujeito (você quer saber quem faz a ação), não entra do / does, e o verbo leva -s como na afirmativa: Who plays the drums in Slipknot? — Eloy Casagrande does. Compare: Who do you like? (De quem você gosta? O sujeito é you.)

Nome de banda: no inglês americano, nome sem plural é it (Does Muse have a new album?); nome no plural, com the, é they (Do the Foo Fighters play in Brazil?).

Gostos, do mais forte para o mais fraco: I love metal. · I really like rock. (Gosto muito de rock.) · I like blues. · Jazz is OK. (Jazz até que é legal.) · I don't like pop very much. (Não gosto muito de pop.) · I don't like country at all. (Não gosto nem um pouco.) · I hate bad lyrics.

Cinco armadilhas de brasileiro

1. "She don't", "Do she...?" → She doesn't like jazz. Does she like Muse? (Em letra de música você ouve "she don't", mas é informal e conta como erro em prova e no trabalho.)

2. -s em dobro. "Does he likes Ghost?", "She doesn't plays": o -s já foi para o does. → Does he like Ghost? She doesn't play.

3. Esquecer o do. "Where you live?", "What means 'lyrics'?" → Where do you live? What does "lyrics" mean?

4. Misturar do e to be. "Do you are a fan?" → Are you a fan? · "I'm not like pop" → I don't like pop.

5. "I like very much rock", "Yes, I like." O very much vai depois da coisa: I like rock very much. (ou I really like rock.) · Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

Leitura: Rock news in five questions (notícias de novembro de 2026)

Do you like rock? Do you follow the music news? Here are five questions about rock in November 2026, with the answers.

1. What does GTA VI have for metal fans? It has The Chamber 106.6, the first metal-only radio station in the GTA series. Its DJs are Tom Araya and Kerry King, from Slayer. Does the station play Brazilian metal? Yes, it does: "Territory", by Sepultura. It also plays "Sober", by Tool, and "Sugar", by System of a Down. And "Mutual Death Warrant", the new song by Power Trip? For now, you only hear it in the game.

2. Does "Mary On A Cross" have a music video? No, it doesn't: this Ghost song is a B-side from 2019. But thanks to TikTok, it now has more than one billion streams on Spotify. And "Square Hammer", Ghost's second most-played song there? Does it have a billion streams too? No, it doesn't. It has 299 million.

3. When do the Foo Fighters play in Brazil? In February 2027. Their Take Cover Tour has about 28 shows until September 2027, and two of them are in Brazil: on February 18, at Arena MRV, in Belo Horizonte, and on February 20, at MorumBIS, in São Paulo. In Brazil, Karen Dió and Deb and The Mentals open the shows. And after the tour? Dave Grohl talks about a break. For him, it seems like the right thing to do. How long is the break? He doesn't know.

4. Does Anthrax's 2027 tour come to South America? No, it doesn't, at least not for now. The band has a new album, Cursum Perficio, its first album in more than ten years. The tour has 19 dates in Europe, from March 14 to April 10, from Dublin to London. Who opens the shows? Sacred Reich and Kerry King, the same Kerry King from the GTA VI radio station!

5. Why does Cheap Trick need a break? We don't know exactly. The band doesn't give details: the members only say that they need a break, and that it isn't about health. After about 50 years on tour, this is the band's first break. Its last show for now is already history: November 22, 2026, in Detroit. Its 2027 dates are canceled. Is there a date for a return? No, there isn't.

And you? Do you like these bands? Which one do you prefer?
